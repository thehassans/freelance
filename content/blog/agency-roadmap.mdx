# Setting Up Your First Agency: A 30-Day Roadmap

Transitioning from a "Full-time Freelancer" to an "Agency Owner" is a psychological shift. You stop being the "doer" and start being the "builder."

## Week 1: The Foundation

Identify your **Niche**. Agencies that do "everything for everyone" die. Agencies that do "Framer sites for B2B SaaS" thrive.

## Week 2: The Systems

Before you hire, you need to document. 
- How do you onboard?
- Where do files live?
- What is the internal QA process?

## Week 3: The First Hire

Don't hire a full-time employee yet. Look for a **white-label partner**. Someone whose work you trust, who can take on the "production" work while you handle strategy and sales.

## Week 4: The Profit Model

If you're paying a contractor $50/hr, you should be charging the client at least $150/hr. 1/3 for the talent, 1/3 for the business overhead/taxes, 1/3 for your profit.

> Success in agency life isn't about working more; it's about making yourself obsolete in the delivery phase.
