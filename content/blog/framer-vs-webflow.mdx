# Profitability Audit: Framer vs. Webflow for Clients

As a freelancer, your time is your most valuable asset. Choosing the right tool isn't just about features—it's about how quickly you can deliver a high-quality result while maintaining healthy margins.

## Framer: The Speed King

If you're moving from Figma, Framer feels like home. 

- **Pros**: Insane build speed, native-feeling animations, easy CMS.
- **When to use**: Creative portfolios, landing pages, marketing sites where "vibe" and "speed" are everything.
- **Profitability**: High. You can likely build 2x faster than Webflow for a similar fee.

## Webflow: The Powerhouse

Webflow remains the standard for complex, scalable corporate sites.

- **Pros**: Logic, high-end CMS, more robust third-party integrations, granular SEO control.
- **When to use**: Tech startups, corporate sites, or any project requiring complex dynamic data.
- **Profitability**: Medium. Build times are longer, but the average project value is typically higher ($10k+).

## The Verdict

For 80% of freelance marketing site projects in 2026, **Framer** is the more profitable choice for the freelancer. However, for "Real Apps" or enterprise-grade scaling, **Webflow** is still the undisputed champion.
