# Retainer

A retainer is one of the best ways to stabilize your freelance income. Instead of hunting for new projects every month, you sell a block of time or a specific set of deliverables to a client on a recurring basis.

## Benefits of Retainers

1. **Predictable Income**: You know exactly how much is coming in next month.
2. **Reduced Sales Effort**: You spend less time pitching and more time doing.
3. **Stronger Relationships**: Being "on call" or deeply embedded in a client's team allows you to provide more value.

## Common Models

- **Time-based**: "20 hours per month for $3,000."
- **Deliverable-based**: "4 blog posts per month for $2,000."
- **Value-based / Access**: "Strategic advisory and availability for $5,000/mo."

## The "Retainer Trap"

Be careful with **rollover hours**. If a client doesn't use their 20 hours this month, do they get 40 next month? Most pros say **no**. Use it or lose it.
