# Scope Creep

Scope creep is the most common reason projects go over budget and over deadline. It occurs when a project starts to include features or tasks that weren't in the original agreement.

## How to spot it
- "While you're at it..."
- "Could we just add one small button?"
- "We've decided we also need a blog section."

## Prevention
The best defense is a highly detailed **Statement of Work (SOW)**. If it's not in the SOW, it's out of scope. When a client asks for more, you acknowledge it and offer a change order: *"Yes, we can do that! It will cost an extra $X and add Y days to the timeline."*
