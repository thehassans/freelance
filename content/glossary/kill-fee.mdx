term: "Kill Fee"
definition: "A payment made to a freelancer if a client cancels a project after work has already begun."

# Kill Fee

A Kill Fee protects your time and opportunity cost. When you commit to a project, you often turn down other work. If the client cancels mid-way, the kill fee ensures you are compensated for that commitment.

## How much should it be?
Typically, kill fees are tiered:
- **Before work starts**: 25% of project total (Deposit)
- **Mid-way**: 50-75% of project total
- **Near completion**: 100% of project total

## How to enforce it
You **must** have a "Termination" clause in your contract that explicitly mentions the kill fee amount and the payment timeline (usually immediate upon cancellation).
