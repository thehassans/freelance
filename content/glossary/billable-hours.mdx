# Billable Hours

Billable hours are the currency of many freelance businesses. If you work for 40 hours a week but only 10 of those were spent on client tasks, you only have 10 billable hours.

## Billable vs. Non-Billable
- **Billable**: Coding, designing, client meetings, research for a specific project.
- **Non-Billable**: Sales calls, administrative work, accounting, learning a new tool, social media marketing.

## Improving Your Billable Ratio
The average freelancer spends 30-40% of their time on non-billable tasks. To increase your income, you have two choices:
1. **Raise your rate**: Charge more for the hours you *do* bill.
2. **Productize your service**: Shift away from hourly billing to package pricing, where the concept of a "billable hour" disappears.
