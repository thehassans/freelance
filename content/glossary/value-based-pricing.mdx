# Value-Based Pricing

Value-based pricing is the "Holy Grail" of freelancing. Instead of selling your hours (which are finite), you sell outcomes.

## Example
- **Hourly**: "I'll spend 10 hours designing this landing page for $1,000."
- **Value-Based**: "This landing page will help you convert 20% more leads. If those leads are worth $100k, my fee for the setup is $15,000."

## How to sell it
You must shift the conversation from **deliverables** (pages, code, logos) to **business goals** (revenue, conversion, speed to market). If you can show a client that your work will generate $X, charging $Y for it feels like a bargain, regardless of how many hours it takes you.
