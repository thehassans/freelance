# Accounts Receivable (AR)

In the freelance world, your Accounts Receivable is the sum total of all your outstanding invoices.

## Why it matters
AR represents cash that belongs to you but isn't in your bank yet. High AR with low bank balance is a common cause of freelance stress.

## Managing AR
1. **Clear Terms**: Always state "Net-XX" terms on your invoice.
2. **Automated Reminders**: Use tools that nudge clients 3 days before and 1 day after a due date.
3. **Upfront Payments**: Reduce your AR by asking for 50% upfront.
